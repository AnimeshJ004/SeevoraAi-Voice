Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Seevora ("we", "our", or "us") provides AI-powered voice receptionist and communication automation services. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you interact with our platform, website, and telephony voice agents.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Details: Name, business email, and organization details provided during registration via Clerk authentication.
  • Communications & Voice Data: Inbound and outbound phone call audio, transcripts, and notes processed on behalf of our business clients.
  • Usage & Telephony Logs: Call durations, timestamps, caller numbers, and billing ledger records.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate, synthesize, and transcribe AI-powered voice agent responses.
  • To process booking confirmations, appointment scheduling, and inquiries.
  • To manage subscriptions, wallets, and billing via payment partners.
  • To comply with regulatory telecommunication and security requirements.

3. Data Protection and Security

All data in transit is protected using industry-standard TLS encryption. API credentials, auth tokens, and telephony keys remain server-side and are never exposed to client browsers. Customers may also configure metadata-only or zero-recording privacy policies.

4. User Data Deletion Rights

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data at any time. For specific instructions on how to request deletion, please see our User Data Deletion Instructions or email us at seevorapvtltd@gmail.com.
