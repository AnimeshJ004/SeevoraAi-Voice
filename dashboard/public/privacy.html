Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Seevora Pvt Ltd ("Seevora", "we", "our", or "us") provides autonomous AI-powered voice receptionists, conversational telephony, and dispatch intelligence. We are deeply committed to protecting the privacy, cryptographic isolation, and telecommunications security of your organization and the callers you serve.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Tenant Details: Name, work email address, company title, and workspace credentials provided during signup via Clerk authentication or direct registration.
  • Telephony & Voice Stream Audio: Inbound and outbound phone audio streams, synthesized voice packets, and live Deepgram speech-to-text transcripts generated strictly for fulfilling automated call flows.
  • Call Analytics & Disposition Metadata: Timestamps, call duration, caller phone numbers, sentiment ratings, and CRM outcome categorizations.
  • Billing & Wallet Records: Transaction identifiers, token allocations, and invoice logs managed through PCI-DSS compliant gateways (Razorpay and PayU).

2. How We Use Information

  • To power sub-second conversational speech synthesis and reasoning with our provider pipeline (Deepgram Nova-3, Groq Qwen, and Rumik Silk).
  • To execute requested real-world actions, such as booking calendar slots on Cal.com, sending confirmation SMS messages, or dispatching customer service tickets.
  • To enforce tenant isolation and role-based access control across multi-tenant enterprise workspaces.
  • To comply with regulatory telecommunication and security requirements mandated by applicable national authorities.

3. Cryptographic Multi-Tenant Isolation & Zero-Recording Mode

Seevora enforces complete tenant segregation: no organization can ever view, query, or access another client's recordings, transcripts, or agent configurations. Additionally, enterprise clients may activate Zero-Recording Privacy Mode from the settings console, which immediately disables all persistent audio file storage on Dograh and retains only ephemeral anonymized analytics.

4. User Data Deletion & Rights

Under Indian Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) and international privacy frameworks, you have the right to inspect, export, or permanently delete all personal data and call recordings associated with your workspace. For self-service deletion, visit your Studio Settings or view our User Data Deletion Instructions.
