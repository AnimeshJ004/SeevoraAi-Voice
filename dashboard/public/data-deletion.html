User Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: November 2, 2026 · Indian DPDP Act 2023 Compliant

Full Cryptographic Erasure Guarantee

Complete zero-trace purge across PostgreSQL, Redis state cache, Deepgram STT buffers, and Dograh telemetry.

Seevora AI Voice complies with the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act and global privacy benchmarks. If you wish to delete your account or any personal records associated with your interaction with Seevora (including phone call audio, transcripts, phone numbers, or account profile), follow the simple instructions below.

1 Self-Service Deletion via Studio Console

You can permanently purge call recordings, transcripts, and contact history directly inside your workspace:

  • Log into your Voice Studio Console.
  • Navigate to Call Recordings & Analytics tab.
  • Click "Purge All Recordings" to delete all existing media and transcripts immediately.
  • To activate Zero-Recording Mode permanently, toggle the option under Tenant Security Settings.

2 Direct Email Data Deletion Request

Alternatively, you can email our Data Protection Officer directly:

Email: seevorapvtltd@gmail.com
Subject: Data Deletion Request
Body details: Registered work email, workspace company name, and phone number.

3 Processing SLA & Confirmation

Once your request is received, our engineering team executes the purge across all relational databases, cold storage archives, and vector memories within 7 business days. An official compliance audit certificate is dispatched to your registered email upon completion.
