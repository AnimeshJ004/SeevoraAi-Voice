User Data Deletion Instructions

Meta Platform Compliant • Last updated: October 12, 2026

Seevora AI Voice complies with Meta Platform terms regarding user data privacy and deletion. If you wish to delete your account or any personal data associated with your interaction with Seevora (including WhatsApp messages, phone numbers, or account profile), follow the instructions below.

Quick Request: You can send a data deletion request directly to seevorapvtltd@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".

How to Request Data Deletion

  1. Send an email from your registered email address to seevorapvtltd@gmail.com.
  2. Include your name, registered phone number (or WhatsApp number), and your organization name.
  3. Specify whether you want to delete:
    • All account data and user profile.
    • Specific call recordings and transcripts.
    • WhatsApp conversation history and contact details.

Processing and Confirmation

Once your request is received, our team will process the permanent deletion from our databases, logs, and storage within 7 business days. You will receive a confirmation email once the deletion is complete.
